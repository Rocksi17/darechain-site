Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your username, avatar, friends, reactions and the videos you posted. If you cannot open the app, email us and we will delete it for you.

How do I report a video?
Tap the menu on the video and choose Report this video. A video reported by several different players is hidden until we review it.

How do I block someone?
Open the menu on one of their videos, or open their profile, and choose Block. You will no longer see their videos or receive their dares. You can unblock them in Settings, Blocked players.

I forgot my password.
Password reset is not available yet. Email us from the address on your account and we will help.

My invite code does not work.
Codes have 6 characters. You can paste a whole message and the app finds the code for you. If the video was removed or hidden, its code stops working.

Who is DareChain for?
DareChain is for ages 13 and over. Please keep dares safe: no dangerous stunts and nothing that could hurt anyone.
