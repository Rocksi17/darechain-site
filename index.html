SAFETY

Fun first. Safety always.

We built DareChain so dares stay fun and nobody gets hurt or bullied.

  • Dares are kept safe. No dangerous stunts, nothing that could hurt anyone.
  • Report and block. One tap on any video. Videos reported by several players are hidden until we review them.
  • You are in control. No real name needed. Delete your account and your videos any time in Settings.
  • Ages 13 and over. Younger players are not allowed.
