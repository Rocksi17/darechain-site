DareChain Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

DareChain ("the app", "we") lets you film a short 10-second dare video, add a short twist, and pass it to friends. This page explains what information the app uses and what choices you have.

Who we are

DareChain is made by Rocksi. You can contact us at rocksirosario@gmail.com.

Who can use DareChain

DareChain is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use the app only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Information we collect

  • Videos and sound. When you record a dare, the video (with its audio) is uploaded to our servers so that the people you share it with can watch it.
  • Twist text. The short text (up to 60 characters) you type when you add your twist.
  • A username and an avatar. You choose a username, plus an emoji and a color for your avatar. You do not need to give your real name.
  • An anonymous account. The app creates this automatically when you open it, so you can use DareChain without signing up.
  • Email and password (optional). If you choose to save your account, we store your email address and a password so you can log in on any phone. The password is stored in a protected (hashed) form by our provider, Supabase. We use the email only for your account.
  • Friends, blocks and dares. The usernames you add as friends, the players you block, and the dares you send and receive.
  • Score information. Your points, level and streak. These are calculated from the videos you send.
  • Reactions. The emoji reactions you give to videos.
  • Chain information. Which dare was used, which video follows which, and the invite code for each video.
  • Reports. If you report a video, we record which video was reported and who reported it.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address and the time of a request, to keep the service running and secure.

We do not ask for your phone number, contacts or location.

Camera and microphone

DareChain asks for camera and microphone access only so you can record your dare. Nothing is recorded unless you tap the record button. You can turn these permissions off at any time in your phone's settings, but you will not be able to record dares without them.

How we use information

  • To show your video and twist to people who have your invite code or who you send a dare to.
  • To show your username, avatar, level and score to other players (see below).
  • To work out points, levels, streaks and the dare of the day.
  • To review reports and remove videos that are harmful or break our rules.
  • To keep the app working, secure and free of abuse.

We do not sell your information. The app currently shows no ads. If we add ads or other services in the future, we will update this policy first.

What other players can see

  • Other players can find you by searching for your username. They can see your username, avatar, level, points, streak, number of dares sent, and the month you joined.
  • Anyone who has your invite code can open your chain and watch your video. Videos are stored behind a web link, so anyone who obtains that exact link may also be able to watch the video.
  • Reactions you give to a video are visible to players who can see that video.
  • Do not record anything private, and only share your invite code with people you trust.

Sharing a video outside the app

The app has a Share button that uses your phone's share menu. When you share a video this way, you choose who receives it, and we cannot control or remove copies once they leave the app.

Services we use

We use Supabase to provide the app's database, video storage and sign-in. Your information is stored on Supabase's servers, which may be located outside your country.

Safety, reporting and blocking

Dares must be safe. Please do not record or pass on dangerous stunts, anything that could hurt you or others, or anything illegal, hateful or inappropriate. If you see a video that breaks these rules, use the menu on the video to report it. You can also block a player so you no longer see their videos or receive their dares. We review reports and may hide or remove videos and block misuse. A video that is reported by several different players may be hidden automatically until we review it.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open Settings and tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your username, avatar, friends, reactions and the videos you posted. Videos that other players recorded when they continued your chain are theirs and stay in their own accounts.

If you cannot use the app, email us at rocksirosario@gmail.com with your username or the invite code of a video you want removed. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Keeping information

We keep videos and account information until they are deleted by you, or removed by us under the rules above.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email rocksirosario@gmail.com.
